Performance Marketing & Paid Acquisition Lead (MENA / GCC)

Location

Cairo or Riyadh

Work Arrangement

Hybrid

Experience Level

L5 / Practice Lead

Compensation Band

USD 2,800 – 4,500 / mo + ROAS Bonus

01 Role Overview & Mission

At Empetix, performance marketers don't operate in the dark. You will sit alongside the software engineers who build our clients' checkout flows and the film directors who shoot our ad creatives—managing USD 150k to 500k/month in paid media budgets across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Europe.

Direct multi-channel paid acquisition (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn), creative testing sprints, and server-side attribution for category-leading brands.

02 Key Responsibilities (What You Will Do)

In your first 90 days and beyond, you will be responsible for:

  • Own full-funnel paid acquisition strategy, budget allocation, and ROAS/CAC targets across Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, and LinkedIn.
  • Collaborate with our backend engineers to configure server-side Conversions API (CAPI), offline conversion imports, and first-party attribution dashboards.
  • Partner with our in-house video and design crew to run weekly structured creative testing sprints (hooks, angles, formats, and landing pages).
  • Analyze cohort retention, LTV:CAC ratios, and funnel drop-off telemetry to scale winning campaigns profitably.
  • Lead client growth reviews with clear, honest financial reporting—zero vanity metrics.

03 Required Qualifications & Requirements

We are looking for candidates who can demonstrate the following qualifications:

  • 4+ years hands-on experience managing performance marketing budgets exceeding USD 50,000/month with verifiable ROAS and CAC case studies.
  • Deep technical command of Meta Business Suite, Google Ads, TikTok/Snap Ads Manager, Google Tag Manager (Server-Side), and GA4.
  • Strong analytical foundation in unit economics (CAC, Payback Period, Contribution Margin, MER, and Cohort LTV).
  • Experience scaling e-commerce, FinTech, real estate, or mobile app campaigns in the GCC and Egyptian markets.
  • Fluency in Arabic and English copy evaluation and creative briefing.

04 Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have)

  • Experience with SQL/BigQuery, Looker Studio, or mobile MMP tools (Adjust / AppsFlyer).
  • Background in conversion rate optimization (A/B testing landing pages).

05 Tools, Stack & Environment

  • Meta Ads & CAPI
  • Google Ads / PMax
  • TikTok & Snap GCC
  • Server-Side Attribution
  • CRO & Unit Economics

06 Compensation, Benefits & Working Model

  • Salary Band: USD 2,800 – 4,500 / mo + ROAS Bonus (benchmarked in USD and paid with zero currency delay).
  • Hardware & Setup: M3 Max MacBook Pro, 4K display, and home-office ergonomic stipend.
  • Health & Time Off: Tier-1 private medical insurance for you and your family + 28 days paid annual leave.
  • Learning Budget: USD 1,800/year for books, courses, certifications, and international conferences.
