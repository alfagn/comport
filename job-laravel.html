Senior Laravel & Distributed Systems Engineer

Location

Cairo or Remote EMEA

Work Arrangement

Hybrid / Remote

Experience Level

L5 / Senior Engineer

Compensation Band

USD 3,500 – 5,200 / mo + Profit Share

01 Role Overview & Mission

As a Senior Laravel & Distributed Systems Engineer at Empetix, you will lead the backend architecture for our flagship FinTech, e-commerce, and logistics platforms. You will work directly alongside CTO Mohanad Ahmed Shehata and a squad of senior product engineers—designing zero-downtime ledger migrations, idempotent webhook processors, and sub-100ms API endpoints.

Architect high-concurrency Laravel 11 APIs, event-sourced FinTech payment ledgers, and multi-tenant SaaS backends serving 1.4M+ active monthly users.

02 Key Responsibilities (What You Will Do)

In your first 90 days and beyond, you will be responsible for:

  • Design, build, and maintain modular Laravel 11 applications using Domain-Driven Design (DDD), strict typing, and event-sourced ledger patterns.
  • Optimize PostgreSQL and Redis query paths to sustain P95 response latencies under 120ms during high-velocity campaign traffic spikes.
  • Integrate regional and global payment gateways, banking webhooks, ERP bridges, and server-side marketing attribution pipelines (Meta CAPI / Google Ads API).
  • Write deterministic automated test suites (Pest / PHPUnit) and lead architectural code reviews across the engineering practice.
  • Mentor mid-level engineers and collaborate with frontend and mobile squads on clean OpenAPI / GraphQL contracts.

03 Required Qualifications & Requirements

We are looking for candidates who can demonstrate the following qualifications:

  • 5+ years of production backend engineering experience, with at least 3+ years building large-scale systems in Laravel and modern PHP (8.2+).
  • Deep proficiency in relational database design (PostgreSQL or MySQL), indexing strategies, transaction isolation levels, and query profiling.
  • Proven experience with asynchronous job queues (Laravel Horizon / Redis / SQS), caching strategies, and webhook idempotency.
  • Strong grasp of automated testing, CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions), Docker containers, and Linux production debugging.
  • Clear written and verbal communication in Arabic and professional English.

04 Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have)

  • Prior experience building FinTech wallets, double-entry accounting ledgers, or high-volume multi-vendor e-commerce checkouts.
  • Familiarity with AWS infrastructure (ECS, RDS, CloudFront, S3) or Terraform.
  • Contributions to open-source Laravel packages or technical writing.

05 Tools, Stack & Environment

  • Laravel 11
  • PHP 8.3
  • PostgreSQL
  • Redis / Horizon
  • AWS ECS
  • DDD / Event Sourcing

06 Compensation, Benefits & Working Model

  • Salary Band: USD 3,500 – 5,200 / mo + Profit Share (benchmarked in USD and paid with zero currency delay).
  • Hardware & Setup: M3 Max MacBook Pro, 4K display, and home-office ergonomic stipend.
  • Health & Time Off: Tier-1 private medical insurance for you and your family + 28 days paid annual leave.
  • Learning Budget: USD 1,800/year for books, courses, certifications, and international conferences.
