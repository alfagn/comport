04 / Engagement FAQ

Common questions before we kick off.

01. Can we hire Empetix for software engineering only, or growth marketing only?

Yes. Around 40% of our clients start with a single practice—such as rebuilding a Laravel/Flutter platform or overhauling paid acquisition and CRO—and expand into a dual-practice partnership once they see the velocity gains of having both teams under one roof.

02. How quickly can an Empetix squad start on our project?

For a 5-Day Architecture & Growth Sprint, we can typically mobilize within 7 to 10 business days. For full dedicated engineering and marketing squads, onboarding takes 2 weeks from signed agreement.

03. Who owns the source code, design tokens, raw video footage, and ad accounts?

You do—100%. All GitHub repositories, Figma design systems, ad accounts (Meta, Google, TikTok), and commercial film footage are provisioned under your organization’s ownership from day one.

04. How do you structure pricing—fixed project fee or monthly retainer?

We offer both: fixed-milestone project pricing for clearly scoped v1.0 platform builds and brand launches, and transparent monthly squad retainers for continuous software delivery, CRO, and performance media scaling.
