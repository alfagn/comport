02 / Operating Principles

How we work when the stakes are high.

01

Code and campaigns share one scoreboard.

We don’t measure engineering in story points and marketing in impressions. Both teams are accountable for activation, retention, and net revenue.

02

Senior principals on the tools.

We don’t bait-and-switch with senior pitch decks and junior execution. Our founders and staff engineers write production code and direct campaigns daily.

03

Arabic and English as equal first-class citizens.

We engineer bidirectional RTL/LTR interfaces and bilingual brand systems natively—never as a rushed translation layer at the end.

04

Full IP ownership and clean handovers.

Every line of Laravel/React code, Figma token, ad account, and raw camera file belongs to our client, documented so your internal team can run with it.
