Behind the Lens: Directing the Qahwa Roasters Omnichannel Launch Shoot

How our in-house film and photography unit shot 42 commercial deliverables in two days—and how pairing tactile 35mm imagery with a custom subscription checkout lifted recurring coffee orders by 190%.

Sara El-Masry

Co-Founder & VP Growth

Production Specs

  • Camera: ARRI Alexa Mini LF & Leica SL2
  • Location: Downtown Cairo Roastery
  • Deliverables: 1 Hero Film, 14 Paid Social Cuts, 28 Product Stills
  • ROAS: 4.2x Blended in Month 1

Designing every camera frame around where it lives in the conversion funnel

Most commercial shoots happen in a vacuum: a production house delivers a cinematic 60-second film, and the performance marketing team is left cropping vertical cuts that don’t fit mobile safe zones or product detail pages.

Because Empetix engineered Qahwa’s subscription e-commerce store AND directed the launch shoot, every frame on our shot list was mapped to a specific slot in the funnel:

  • Top-of-funnel 9:16 hooks: Tactile close-ups of espresso extraction and ceramic craftsmanship timed to a 1.8-second visual hook window.
  • Interactive Flavor Quiz visuals: Overhead macro plates of roast profiles integrated directly into our custom React subscription configurator.
  • Retargeting founder vignettes: Candid documentary interviews with the head roaster addressing freshness and delivery speed.
